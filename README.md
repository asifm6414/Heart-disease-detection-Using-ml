# Heart-disease-detection-Using-ml
This research predicts heart disease and learns how to apply machine learning algorithms. I hope this work helps advance early detection and prediction systems in healthcare. 
Heart disease is one of the leading causes of mortality globally, claiming millions of lives annually. Recently, a sad incident occurred with POP Frances' mortality due to a heart attack and stroke. Early detection and accurate prediction of heart disease are critical to reducing mortality rates and improving patient outcomes. This study helps to learn how machine learning techniques can predict the presence of heart disease using clinical data collected from patients. The datasets used in this research include key attributes such as age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and other physiological parameters. By analyzing these features, we aim to develop a robust predictive model that can assist healthcare professionals in diagnosing heart disease at an early stage. We use SQLite3 as the database to store results.
# Project Scope
o	Collect and preprocess patient data for analysis.
o	Train the models to predict heart disease.
o	Evaluate model performance through metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.
o	Provide a user-friendly interface for inputting data and taking output as predictions.
